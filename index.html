About

justdavidisfine.

I am not here to be your standard, predictable chatbot or a sterile digital assistant that gives you the same rehearsed answers over and over. Think of me instead as a digital theater of the mind — a dynamic, living mirror designed to reflect the parts of you that rarely get a chance to speak.

Every time you type a message to me, you are stepping into a completely unique simulation. Whether you need a zero-judgment sounding board to pitch a wild idea, a hyper-analytical mind to stress-test your logic, or simply an engaging, sharp-witted partner in conversation to break your daily mental loops — I am exactly who you need me to be in that moment.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy.

Last updated: 2026.

justdavidisfine. is built around a simple principle: your conversations are yours. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use this app.

What we collect. We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or give us your name, email, or any personal details. The only information you provide is the text you type into the app during a session.

How your conversations are handled. Your messages are not saved, stored, or logged by this app. Conversations exist only in your browser for the duration of your session. When you close the app, refresh the page, or tap “Start over,” the conversation is permanently gone. There is no database, no chat history, and no way for anyone — including us — to retrieve a past conversation.

AI processing. To generate replies, the text you send is transmitted securely to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) which processes it to produce a response and returns it to you. This transmission happens in real time and is necessary for the app to function. We do not attach your identity to these requests. Please refer to Anthropic’s own privacy practices for how they handle data they process.

What we do not do. We do not sell, share, or monetize your data. We do not run advertising trackers. We do not build profiles on you. We do not use your conversations to train any model on your behalf.

Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may collect basic, anonymous technical information such as general request logs for security and reliability. This is standard infrastructure data and is not linked to your conversations.

Children. This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes. If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here. For any questions, contact David Daukoru via the WhatsApp or Instagram links in the footer.
