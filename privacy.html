Privacy Policy

Your privacy.

Last updated: June 6, 2026

justdavidisfine. is built around a simple principle: your conversations are yours. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use this application.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or any personal details to use the app. The only information you provide is the text you choose to type into the app during a session.

How Your Conversations Are Handled

Your messages are not saved, stored, or logged by this app. Conversations exist only in your device's browser memory for the duration of your active session. When you close the app, refresh the page, or tap “Start over,” the conversation is permanently gone. There is no database, no chat history, and no way for anyone — including us — to retrieve a past conversation.

AI Processing

To generate replies, the text you send is transmitted securely to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) which processes it in real time to produce a response and returns it to you. This transmission is necessary for the app to function. We do not attach your identity to these requests. Please refer to Anthropic’s own privacy practices for how they handle data they process on their systems.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because this app does not collect or store any personal data, there is no personal information retained on our systems and therefore nothing for us to delete. Each session is self-contained and disappears the moment it ends. You can clear any active conversation instantly at any time by tapping “Start over” or by closing the app. As we hold no user records, there is no data deletion request process required — your data is never retained in the first place.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell, share, or monetize your data. We do not run advertising trackers. We do not build profiles on you. We do not use your conversations to train any AI model.

Technical Data

Like most websites, our hosting provider may collect basic, anonymous technical information such as standard server request logs, used solely for security and reliability. This is routine infrastructure data and is not linked to your conversations or your identity.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or your privacy, you can contact us at david.daukoru@gmail.com. You may also reach David Daukoru through the Instagram (@justdavidisfine) or WhatsApp links provided in the app.
